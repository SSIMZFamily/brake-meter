How to use: mount the phone firmly on the dashboard, screen facing you. Tap Start, allow motion access, then tap Calibrate while the car is stopped or cruising steadily. In Auto mode the forward direction is set from your first clear pull-away — if that first move was actually a brake, tap Flip ↕. In Portrait / Landscape mode the direction is set from the mount itself. Keep the phone still in its mount while driving.

Privacy: everything is measured and stored on this phone only. No network requests, no accounts, no tracking.

Uber standard (CMT)

Uber counts an event only when it lasts at least 0.6 s. Braking 0.33 g · Accel 0.31 g · Turns 0.43 g. Brief bumps don’t count.

How to use: mount the phone firmly on the dashboard, screen facing you. Tap Start Trip, allow motion and location access, then tap Calibrate while the car is stopped or cruising steadily. In Auto mode the forward direction is set from your first clear pull-away — if that first move was actually a brake, tap Flip ↕. The app also cross-checks the direction against GPS: if your speed is rising but the meter reads braking, it warns you the direction looks flipped. Tap End Trip when you're done and the trip saves to your history below; the Today card totals the whole day. Background: iPhone stops web apps when you switch to another app, so recording pauses while you're away and resumes when you come back — nothing is recorded in between. Keep the phone charging — GPS uses extra battery. Drive to the Uber standard: brake early and gently, build speed smoothly, and slow down before turns — if this meter stays quiet, Uber’s should too.

Privacy: everything is measured and stored on this phone only — trips, speed and location never leave the device. No network requests, no accounts, no tracking.
